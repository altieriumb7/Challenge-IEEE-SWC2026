The Mathematical Engine

Governing Equations

1. Local Control: Webster HCM Uniform Delay

$$ d_{uniform} = \frac{C(1-\lambda)^2}{2(1-\min(\lambda x, 0.99))} $$

Where $x = q / (\lambda \cdot S)$ is the Volume-to-Capacity ratio.

2. Spatial Diffusion: Water-filling Routing

$$ \Delta v_i = - \phi \cdot (v_i - \bar{v}_{neighbors}) \cdot \frac{lanes_n}{\sum lanes_{neighbors}} $$

Distributes excess traffic $v_i$ proportionally to neighboring lane capacity.

3. AI Optimization: Continuous Reward Function

$$ R = - \alpha (\max E_{vuln}) - \beta (\sum E_{total}) - \gamma (\bar{W}_{global}) - \delta (\max W_{city}) $$

The PPO Agent maximizes $R$ to find the Pareto optimal point between Emissions ($E$) and Wait times ($W$).
